## Brand Name
**MD** = Medical · **Ops** = Operations — a modern HealthTech company focused on optimising hospital operations.

## Tagline
- **Primary:** Intelligent Healthcare Operations · عمليات رعاية صحية ذكية
- Approved alternates: "Healthcare Operations. Simplified." · "Powering Modern Hospitals." · "Smarter Operations. Better Care."

## Brand Promise
Less time on administration. More time for patients. · وقت أقل في الإدارة، ووقت أكثر للمرضى.

## Vision & Mission
- **Vision:** To power the operations of every modern hospital.
- **Mission:** To make healthcare operations effortless — helping hospitals work more efficiently, decide with confidence, and give more of their time back to patients.

## Positioning
MDops is an enterprise healthcare operations platform that lets hospitals manage workforce scheduling, perioperative operations, analytics, approvals, and communication from one secure system.

## Brand Personality
Intelligent, professional, modern, innovative, secure, premium, calm, reliable, human-centred, enterprise-grade. Never playful, never childish, never overly clinical.

## Values
Innovation · Reliability · Security · Simplicity · Excellence · Partnership

## Typography, Logo & Photography
Clean modern sans-serif (premium enterprise feel). The mark is a rising "ascend" signal; the wordmark carries a pulse "o". Icons minimal and geometric. Photography: real hospitals and teams, natural light — never staged stock.

## Product Ecosystem
| Product | What it does |
|---|---|
| MDops OR | Operating-room & perioperative management — specialty-aware placement, slot locking, distribute-then-publish |
| MDops Workforce | Monthly scheduling, leave & approvals, holidays, working-hour compliance |
| MDops OnCall | On-call generation with fairness tracking and post-call rest |
| MDops Analytics | Executive KPI centre — utilisation, staffing health, fairness |
| MDops AI | Fair, vacation-aware, rule-compliant scheduling in one click |
| MDops Mobile | Assignments, leave requests, and alerts anywhere |
| MDops Command | The department's live status in one executive view |

## Target Audience
- **Buyers:** hospital CEOs/COOs, medical directors & department chairs, OR & anaesthesia heads.
- **Daily users:** coordinators/schedulers, consultants, fellows, residents, technologists, nursing & perioperative leadership.
- **Organisations:** private, government, and military hospitals; academic medical centres; day-surgery centres.

## Technology & Security
Cloud-native, secure multi-tenant SaaS. Per-organisation isolated data environment with a dedicated web address; tenant isolation at the database level; 2FA; session integrity; least-privilege RBAC; encryption in transit; per-organisation file storage; data-residency options. Aligned with Saudi PDPL and comparable international standards. Bilingual EN/AR with RTL, web and mobile.

1. Discovery → 2. Provisioning → 3. Configuration → 4. Pilot → 5. Rollout → 6. Optimisation
